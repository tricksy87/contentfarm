Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers Content Farm, the private studio tool used by the Content Farm studio ("we", "us") to publish our own videos, and this website.

Who uses Content Farm

Content Farm is used only by us, on our own computer, to post to social media accounts we own (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook). It is not available to the public and does not collect information about other people.

What information it accesses

When we connect one of our own accounts, the platform (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta) gives Content Farm an access token and basic account details such as the account name, ID and follower count. Content Farm uses these only to upload our videos, set their captions, and read our own posts' statistics (views, likes, comments) for our reporting.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and account details are stored encrypted on our own computer. They are not sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Google and YouTube data

Content Farm's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy also apply. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok and Meta data

TikTok and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) data is used only to publish our own content and read our own posts' statistics. Access can be removed at any time in each app's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Facebook: Settings → Business integrations).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep tokens only while an account is connected. Removing an account's credential in Content Farm deletes its stored token. To ask about or delete any data, email tricksy87@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
