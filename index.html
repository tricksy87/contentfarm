Content Farm

Content Farm is a small independent studio. We make original narrated story videos (true crime, horror, fantasy and more) with illustrated scenes, and publish them on our own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook channels, such as JacksMurders.

The Content Farm app

The Content Farm app is our private studio tool. It runs on our own computer, prepares each story video and posts it to our own social media accounts through each platform's official API. It is not offered to the public and only connects to accounts we own.
